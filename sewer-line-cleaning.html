How Sewer Line Cleaning Runs in Arlington Heights

Arlington Heights sits on a mix of old clay tile laterals and mid-century cast iron, and both fail in predictable ways. The freeze-thaw cycle each winter pushes roots into hairline cracks, and by spring you have a slow drain that turns into a backup. We see it every year in Scarsdale and Northgate, where mature maples and elms sit close to the sewer runs.

Our team uses a RIDGID sewer camera to inspect the line before jetting, so we know whether we are dealing with root intrusion, grease buildup or a collapsed section. If the pipe is sound, we jet it with a high-pressure hydro jetter and a root-cutting nozzle. If it is not, we tell you before we do more damage.
