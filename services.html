Plumbing Services Arlington Heights, Illinois

One truck, one call, and the whole job handled. We cover everything from a running toilet to a full sewer line replacement, and we stock the parts that matter: copper pipe, PEX tubing, PVC pipe, supply lines, and shut-off valves.

  • Emergency Plumbing Services

    Burst pipe, sewer backup, or gas smell at midnight? We run emergency calls across Arlington Heights with a stocked truck and shut the problem down fast.

  • Plumbing Repairs

    Dripping faucets, leaking supply lines, and failing shut-off valves are the small repairs that turn into big water damage.

  • Drain Cleaning

    A slow kitchen drain or gurgling shower usually means buildup or root intrusion down the line.

  • Sewer Line Cleaning

    When multiple drains back up at once, the main sewer line is the culprit.

  • Sewer Line Repair

    Cracked or offset sewer pipe causes repeat backups and soggy yards.

  • Sewer Line Replacement

    When a sewer line is collapsed or root-choked beyond patching, replacement is the honest answer. We run new PVC pipe, set proper slope, and tie into the city main.

  • Water Heater Repair

    No hot water, rusty water, or popping sounds usually point to a failed element, thermostat, or a water heater anode rod that is long gone.

  • Water Heater Installation

    We install tank water heaters sized to your household, set the temperature, check the venting, and haul the old unit away.

  • Tankless Water Heater Services

    Navien and other tankless units need annual descaling to keep flow rates up. We service, repair, and install tankless systems, flush the heat exchanger, and check gas pressure.

  • Faucet Repair and Installation

    A dripping Moen or Delta faucet wastes water and stains the sink. We replace cartridges, seats, and supply lines, or install a new faucet you picked out.

  • Toilet Repair and Installation

    A running toilet can waste hundreds of gallons a day. We replace fill valves, flappers, and wax rings, fix wobbling bowls, and install new toilets with a proper seal.

  • Shower and Bathtub Services

    Leaking shower valves, clogged tub drains, and loose fixtures are common calls.

  • Sink and Garbage Disposal Services

    A jammed or leaking disposal is usually a quick fix, not a replacement. We clear jams, reseal flanges, and install new units when the motor is done.

  • Sump Pump Services

    Sump pump failure during a spring storm floods basements fast. We install, repair, and test Zoeller and other sump pumps, check float switches, and add battery backup.

  • Water Softener Installation and Repair

    Hard water scales up water heaters and clogs fixtures across Cook County. We install and repair water softeners, set regeneration cycles, and test hardness before and after.

  • Water Filtration System Services

    Whole-home and under-sink filtration removes chlorine, sediment, and taste issues. We install and service filtration systems, change cartridges on schedule, and check pressure drop.

  • Gas Line Services

    New gas ranges, dryers, and outdoor grills need a proper gas line run. We install and extend gas lines with code-correct pipe and fittings, pressure-test every connection, and coordinate inspection.

  • Gas Leak Detection and Repair

    If you smell gas, leave and call us. We use leak detection equipment to pinpoint the source, shut off the supply, and repair or replace the failed section.

  • Pipe Repair and Replacement

    Frozen pipes, pinhole leaks, and corroded galvanized lines cause most of the pipe repairs we see.

  • Repiping Services

    When pipes are corroded throughout, spot repairs just delay the next leak.

  • Leak Detection and Repair

    A slab leak or hidden wall leak shows up as a high water bill or a warm floor.

  • Hydro Jetting

    Hydro jetting scours grease, scale, and roots from drain lines with high-pressure water. It is the right call for recurring commercial kitchen clogs and stubborn main lines.

  • Video Camera Inspection

    A sewer camera shows exactly what is happening inside your pipe: cracks, roots, bellies, or blockages.

  • Backflow Prevention and Testing

    Illinois requires annual backflow testing on many commercial and irrigation systems. Our ASSET-certified testers inspect, test, and file the paperwork, and we repair or replace failed assemblies.

  • Bathroom Plumbing Services

    From a leaking shower valve to a full bathroom remodel rough-in, we handle the plumbing side start to finish. We set drains, run supply lines, install fixtures, and test everything.

  • Kitchen Plumbing Services

    Kitchen calls cover sink clogs, disposal failures, dishwasher hookups, and faucet replacements. We snake drains, reseal traps, and install new fixtures with new supply lines and shut-off valves.

  • Laundry Room Plumbing Services

    Washing machine supply lines and drain pans are a top source of hidden water damage. We replace worn hoses, install proper standpipes and pans, and clear slow laundry drains.

  • Commercial Plumbing Services

    Restaurants, offices, and retail spaces in the Arlington Heights Business District need plumbing that keeps up.

  • Residential Plumbing Services

    Houses, condos, and townhomes across Arlington Heights get the same treatment: honest diagnosis, code-correct work, and a clean job site.

  • Plumbing Maintenance

    An annual once-over catches failing shut-off valves, corroded supply lines, and water heater anode rods before they cause damage. We flush the water heater, test pressure, and check for leaks.

How Our Plumbing Service Works

You call, we answer, and we tell you what happens next. No phone-tree runaround, no vague arrival windows. Here is the process from first call to final test.

  1. Call And Describe

    Tell us what is happening: no hot water, water backing up, a leak you can see. We ask a few questions to figure out urgency and send the right tech with the right parts.

  2. Diagnose On Site

    We inspect the problem, use a pressure gauge or sewer camera where needed, and explain what we found in plain language. You see the issue, not just hear about it.

  3. Approve The Price

    You get a written price for the repair before we start. If you want to think it over, that is fine. No work happens until you say go.

  4. Repair And Test

    We complete the repair with code-correct materials, then test under pressure or run water to confirm the fix. We clean up the work area and walk you through what was done.
