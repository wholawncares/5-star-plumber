Seasonal Plumbing Considerations in Northgate

Northgate's climate means plumbing needs change with the seasons. Winter brings frozen pipes and high demand for emergency service. Spring thaws can reveal leaks and cause sump pump failures. Summer is a good time for maintenance and repiping. Fall is the time to winterize.

  • Spring

    • Spring thaws can cause soil movement and pipe shifts, leading to leaks. Sump pumps work overtime to keep basements dry. We see an increase in sump pump repairs and sewer backups.
    • Test your sump pump by pouring water into the pit. If it does not turn on, call us for a repair or replacement.

  • Summer

    • Summer is a busy time for plumbing repairs and remodels. Water heaters work harder, and outdoor plumbing like hose bibs and irrigation lines can leak.
    • Schedule a plumbing maintenance visit to check for leaks and flush your water heater before the busy season.

  • Fall

    • As temperatures drop, you should winterize your plumbing. Disconnect hoses, insulate exposed pipes, and shut off outdoor water lines. Frozen pipes become a risk in late fall.
    • Insulate pipes in unheated areas like garages and crawl spaces. We can install heat tape for added protection.

  • Winter

    • Winter is our busiest season for emergency calls. Frozen pipes burst and cause water damage. Water heaters fail when they work hardest.
    • Keep your thermostat at 55 degrees or higher, even when you are away. Open cabinet doors under sinks to let warm air circulate.
