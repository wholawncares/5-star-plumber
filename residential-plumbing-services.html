Methods

  • Soldering torch and pipe cutter

    Copper repairs need clean cuts and tight sweat joints. We solder copper pipe with a torch and pressure-test every joint before closing the wall.

  • Pressure gauge

    We measure static and dynamic water pressure at the main and at fixtures to diagnose low pressure and check regulator settings.

  • Camera-first diagnosis

    Before we snake or jet a drain, we camera it. That way we know whether the blockage is grease, roots or a broken pipe, and we do not sell you a cleaning when the real problem is a repair.

  • Pressure testing

    Every gas line and water line repair gets pressure-tested before we call it done. If a joint weeps under test, we fix it then, not after you find a wet ceiling.

  • Code-correct materials

    We use copper pipe, PEX tubing, PVC pipe and ABS pipe as the application calls for, with the right fittings, solder, Teflon tape and plumber's putty. No shortcuts that fail inspection.

  • Backflow testing to ASSET standards

Repair or replace: how to decide

Age, material and how often the same line fails tell you whether a repair holds or you are throwing money at a dying system.

Repair

A single failure on otherwise sound piping

  • One leaking joint
  • Fixture part worn out
  • Line under 15 years

Partial replace

A section that keeps failing while the rest holds

  • Repeated pinhole leaks
  • One damaged sewer section
  • Root intrusion at a joint

Full replace

A system at the end of its service life

  • Galvanized lines failing
  • Tank past 12 years
  • Collapsed sewer lateral
