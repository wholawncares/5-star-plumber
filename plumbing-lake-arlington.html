How Seasons Affect Plumbing in Lake Arlington

Lake Arlington's climate shapes plumbing demand across the year. Winter brings frozen pipe risk, spring brings flooding and sump pump calls, summer is water heater and irrigation season, and fall is the time to prep for cold. Planning around those cycles saves money and prevents damage.

  • Spring

    • Spring storms and snowmelt raise the water table and push water into sump pits and foundation drains. Sewer backups and sump pump failures are the top calls in the lakeside residential area.
    • Test your sump pump with a bucket of water before the first big rain, and clear the pit of debris. If the pump hesitates, call us.

  • Summer

    • Summer is water heater and irrigation season. Sediment and hard water shorten tank life, and backflow testing for irrigation systems comes due. Outdoor line leaks also show up.
    • Flush your water heater and check the anode rod. If your irrigation system needs backflow testing, schedule it before the deadline.

  • Fall

    • Fall is prep season. Exterior pipes need insulation, and shut-off valves should be checked before the first freeze. Sump pumps get a final test before winter.
    • Insulate exposed pipes in the garage and crawl space, and disconnect garden hoses. A frozen hose bib can split the pipe behind the wall.

  • Winter

    • Hard freezes bring frozen pipe and burst pipe calls, especially in older homes with exterior wall runs. Water heater failures also spike when the tank works harder.
    • Keep the heat on, open cabinet doors on exterior walls, and let a faucet drip during extreme cold. If a pipe freezes, call us before it thaws and splits.
