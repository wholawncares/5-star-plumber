How Westgate Seasons Affect Your Plumbing

Plumbing in Westgate follows the seasons. Winter brings frozen pipe calls, spring brings sump pump and sewer backups, summer is water heater and outdoor spigot season, and fall is the time to check shut-off valves before the cold hits. Here is what to expect and what to do each season.

  • Spring

    • Thawing ground and heavy rain put pressure on sump pumps and sewer laterals. Basement backups and sump pump failures are common in the Dundee Road area and other low-lying blocks.
    • Test your sump pump before the first big rain. Pour a bucket of water into the pit and make sure the pump kicks on and clears the water.

  • Summer

    • Water heater replacements and outdoor spigot repairs pick up. Sediment and scale catch up with older tanks, and outdoor faucets that froze over winter start leaking.
    • Flush your water heater once a year to clear sediment. It helps efficiency and extends the tank's life.

  • Fall

    • This is the time to check shut-off valves and exposed pipes before freezing weather. Valves that have not been turned in years often fail when you need them most.
    • Turn your main shut-off valve open and closed once to make sure it works. If it is stiff or will not close, have it replaced before winter.

  • Winter

    • Frozen pipes and burst supply lines are the emergency calls. Exterior walls, crawlspaces and garages are the vulnerable spots. A split pipe can flood a basement when it thaws.
    • Keep cabinet doors open on exterior walls during cold snaps and let a trickle run from vulnerable faucets. If a pipe freezes, call us before you try to thaw it with a torch.
