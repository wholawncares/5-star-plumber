Common Plumbing Problems in Sherwood

Sherwood's plumbing problems come from three things: the age of the housing stock, the mature tree canopy, and the hard water that runs through Cook County. Add cold winters and you get a predictable list of calls. Here is what we see most often and how we handle it.

  • Root intrusion in clay sewer laterals

    Sherwood has a mature tree canopy, and many homes still have original clay sewer laterals with joint gaps. Roots find the moisture, enter through the gaps, and grow into a mass that catches everything you send down the drain.

  • Frozen and burst pipes in exterior walls

    Sherwood winters drop below 20 degrees regularly. Pipes in unheated crawl spaces and exterior walls freeze, expand, and split. The leak usually shows up in the ceiling below after the thaw, not during the freeze.

  • Galvanized supply lines corroding from the inside

    Homes built in the 1950s and 1960s in Sherwood still have galvanized steel supply lines. They rust internally, which restricts flow and discolors the water. Eventually the pipe pinholes and leaks inside a wall.

  • Water heater failures during cold snaps

    Cold incoming water makes a water heater work harder, and sediment at the bottom of the tank insulates the burner from the water. The combination shortens tank life, and failures cluster in December and January.

How Seasons Affect Plumbing in Sherwood

Plumbing in Sherwood follows the calendar. Winter is emergencies, spring is water, summer is drains, and fall is preparation. Knowing what is coming helps you stay ahead of it. If you need emergency drain cleaning Sherwood, call us any time of year.

  • Spring

    • Snowmelt and spring rain push groundwater against foundations, and sump pumps run constantly. Sewer laterals with root intrusion back up more often because the soil is saturated and the roots are actively growing.
    • Test your sump pump by pouring water into the pit and watching it discharge. If it hesitates or runs without moving water, call before the next storm.

  • Summer

    • Drain calls pick up as kitchens and bathrooms get heavy use. Water heater failures also show up in summer because the tank has been working hard all winter and the sediment layer has built up.
    • Flush your water heater once this summer. It removes sediment, extends tank life, and keeps your hot water recovery fast.

  • Fall

    • This is the season to get ahead of winter. Exterior spigots, crawl space pipes, and sump pump discharge lines all need attention before the first hard freeze. Water heater repair in Sherwood IL books up fast in October and November.
    • Disconnect garden hoses, shut off and drain exterior spigots, and insulate any pipe in an unheated space before the first freeze.

  • Winter

    • Frozen pipes and burst lines dominate the call volume. Water heater failures spike. Sewer backups happen too, because cold slows the bacterial action in the line and grease solidifies faster.
    • Know where your main shut-off valve is and make sure it turns. If a pipe freezes, shut off the water before you thaw it, and never use an open flame.
