Tools and Methods We Use for Kitchen Plumbing

Move across a circle, or focus it and press Enter, for an illustrative thermal view of the photograph.

  • Pipe Wrench and Tube Cutter

    Used to loosen and tighten pipe fittings and cut copper or PEX tubing to length. Essential for supply line repairs and faucet swaps.

  • Drain Snake and Auger

    Clears clogs in kitchen drain lines and P-traps. We use a hand snake for small clogs and a powered auger for tougher blockages in the branch line.

  • Hydro Jetter

    Blasts grease and sludge out of kitchen drain lines with high-pressure water. Safer for pipes than chemical cleaners and more thorough than snaking alone.

  • Sewer Camera

    Lets us see inside your drain line to find roots, breaks, or buildup. We show you the footage so you understand what is causing the backup.

  • PEX Crimping Tool

    Used to make secure connections on PEX tubing. PEX is flexible, resists corrosion, and is ideal for kitchen remodels where lines need to be rerouted.

Kitchen Plumbing Problems We Fix in Arlington Heights

Kitchen plumbing issues range from annoying to urgent. A dripping faucet wastes water. A backed-up drain shuts down your kitchen. A leaking supply line can damage your cabinets. Here are the most common problems we solve for Arlington Heights homeowners.

  • Sink leakKitchen sink leaking into the cabinet belowWater rots the cabinet floor, warps the wood, and can lead to mold. We find the source, whether it is a loose slip nut, a cracked tailpiece, or a failed basket strainer. Then we replace the damaged part, tighten the connections, and test the drain. Most sink leaks fix in under an hour.
  • Disposal jamGarbage disposal hums but will not drainA jammed disposal stops your kitchen workflow and can burn out the motor if you keep running it. Food sits in the chamber and starts to smell. You are stuck washing dishes in the bathroom sink. We cut power, free the jam with an auger, and reset the unit. If the motor is burned out, we install a new disposal the same day. We also check the drain line for clogs that may have caused the jam.
  • Slow drainSlow or clogged kitchen drainGrease and food buildup narrows the pipe until water backs up into the sink. Chemical cleaners can corrode the pipes and make the problem worse. You end up with standing water and a bad smell. We clear the clog with a drain snake or hydro jetter, depending on the cause. Then we run a camera if needed to check for roots or pipe damage. You get full flow restored and advice on keeping it clear.
  • Low pressureLow water pressure at the kitchen faucetA clogged aerator or failing shut-off valve reduces flow. You stand at the sink waiting for the water to fill a pot. If the valve is failing, it may leak or burst under pressure. We remove and clean the aerator, test the shut-off valves, and check the supply lines. If the valve is bad, we replace it with a new quarter-turn valve. Most pressure issues fix in under an hour.
  • Dishwasher backupDishwasher not draining or backing up into the sinkA clogged dishwasher drain line sends dirty water into your sink. The dishwasher leaves dishes gritty and the kitchen smells. If the air gap or disposal inlet is blocked, the problem repeats. We check the drain hose, air gap, and disposal inlet. Then we clear the blockage and test the cycle. If the disposal is the issue, we repair or replace it. You get clean dishes and a dry sink.
