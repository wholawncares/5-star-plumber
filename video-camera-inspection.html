A video camera inspection Arlington Heights IL is a diagnostic service that runs a flexible camera head through your drain or sewer line and records the inside of the pipe on a monitor. Sewer Camera Inspection Arlington Heights: Problems We Find

Most camera calls start with a symptom the homeowner can see: a backup, a gurgle, or a slow drain that keeps returning. The camera finds the cause behind the symptom.

  • Root intrusionRoots invading clay tile jointsRoots grow into the joint gaps, catch paper and grease, and cause repeat backups that snaking only temporarily clears. Left alone, roots can crack the pipe. We camera the line, measure the root mass location, and cut it out with a sewer machine or hydro jetter. If the joint is damaged, we quote a spot repair at the measured distance.
  • Collapsed sectionCollapsed or bellied sewer sectionA belly holds water and debris, so the line clogs even after cleaning. A collapsed section can cause sewage to back up into the basement. The camera shows the belly or collapse and its distance from the cleanout. We explain whether spot repair or sewer line replacement is the honest fix and price it from the footage.
  • Cracked fittingCracked PVC pipe at a fittingA cracked fitting leaks sewage into the soil and lets roots in. The clog keeps returning because the pipe cannot carry waste properly. We locate the crack on camera, mark the distance, and repair the section with new PVC pipe and fittings. The line is tested before we leave.
  • Scale buildupScale buildup narrowing the pipeMineral scale and grease reduce the pipe's inside diameter, so the drain is slow and backs up easily. Cleaning helps, but the scale returns without a plan. The camera shows how much of the pipe is narrowed. We recommend hydro jetting or sewer line cleaning and explain how often the line should be checked.
