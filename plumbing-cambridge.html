Seasonal Plumbing Considerations in Cambridge

Cambridge's four seasons each bring different plumbing concerns. From spring rains to winter freezes, we're ready to help. Here's what to expect and how to prepare.

  • Spring

    • Spring rains can overwhelm sump pumps and cause basement flooding. We also see an increase in sump pump repairs and installations.
    • Test your sump pump by pouring water into the pit. If it doesn't turn on, call us for a replacement.

  • Summer

    • Summer is a good time for plumbing maintenance. We often handle repiping and water heater replacements when schedules are flexible.
    • Schedule a plumbing inspection to catch small issues before they become big problems.

  • Fall

    • As temperatures drop, we get calls for frozen pipe prevention. We also service furnaces and water heaters before winter.
    • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets. Insulate exposed pipes in your basement or crawl space.

  • Winter

    • Winter is our busiest season for emergency calls. Frozen pipes, burst pipes, and water heater failures are common.
    • Keep your thermostat at a consistent temperature and open cabinet doors to let warm air circulate around pipes.
