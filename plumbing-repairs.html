What our plumbing repair service includes

Every repair visit starts with a diagnosis and ends with a tested, working fixture or line. We tell you what is included before we start and what might be a separate repair if we find something else.

Tools and methods we use

  • Sewer camera

    We run a camera through drain and sewer lines to see cracks, root intrusion, and blockages before we repair. You see the footage and understand the diagnosis.

  • Leak detection equipment

    Acoustic and pressure-based tools pinpoint hidden leaks in slabs, walls, and underground lines. Finding the leak first keeps the repair small.

    High-pressure water scours grease, scale, and roots from pipe walls. It restores full flow in drains and sewer laterals without harsh chemicals.

    Hand tools for tightening fittings, removing fixtures, and clearing simple clogs. Every truck carries them for fast fixes.

Methods

  • Soldering torch and pipe cutter

    For copper pipe repairs, we cut clean and solder leak-free joints. Proper technique matters on pressurized supply lines.

  • Pressure gauge

    We measure static and dynamic water pressure to diagnose restrictions, leaks, and regulator failures. It tells us whether the problem is supply or fixture.

  • Illinois Plumbing License standards
