What Our Toilet Service Covers

Every visit includes a full inspection, the repair or install itself, testing, and cleanup. Here is what is standard on a toilet repair and installation Arlington Heights IL job, and what we handle as an add-on if we find it.

  • Full Fixture Inspection

    We check the flapper, fill valve, flush valve seal, tank bolts, and supply line before quoting. On a replacement, we inspect the flange and the floor around the drain for rot or cracks.

  • Repair or Rebuild

    A rebuild kit covers the flapper, fill valve, and seal for a fraction of a new toilet. We install Moen, Delta, and American Standard kits and adjust the water level to the marked line.

  • New Toilet Installation

    We set the new bowl on a fresh wax ring, shim it level, torque the bolts, and connect the supply line. A standard install runs 45 to 90 minutes, and we haul the old toilet away.

  • Flange and Valve Repair

    A cracked flange or a seized shut-off valve turns a simple swap into a bigger job. We repair the flange and replace the valve with copper pipe or PEX tubing and new fittings.

  • Leak and Flush Testing

    We flush three times, check the base for seepage, and watch the tank for ghost filling. If the fill valve hisses or the bowl rocks, we correct it before we leave.

  • Cleanup and Haul-Away

    Drop cloths protect the floor, and we wipe down the fixture and surrounding area. The old toilet and packaging leave with us at no extra charge.
