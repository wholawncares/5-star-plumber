What our drain cleaning service includes

Every drain cleaning call includes the basics: access to the line, clearing the blockage, and confirming flow before we leave. Add-ons like hydro jetting or camera inspection are quoted separately when the line needs them. You always know what is included before we start.

Drain cleaning Arlington Heights IL is the process of removing grease, hair, soap scum, food waste, and root intrusion from the pipes that carry wastewater out of your home or business. Tools and methods we use on drains

Move across a circle, or focus it and press Enter, for an illustrative thermal view of the photograph.

  • RIDGID drain snake and auger

    Used to break up and pull out blockages in branch lines and main sewers. The right head depends on whether the clog is grease, hair, or roots.

  • Hydro jetter

    High-pressure water scours grease, sludge, and roots off the pipe walls. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe, which a snake cannot do.

  • Sewer camera

    Shows what is inside the line before and after cleaning. We use it to find roots, cracks, bellies, and offset joints that cause repeat clogs.

  • Milwaukee auger

    Cordless auger for tight spaces like under-sink traps and toilet flanges. It clears the clog without damaging the fixture.

  • Pipe wrench and tube cutter

    Used to open cleanouts, remove trap assemblies, and cut out damaged sections when a repair is needed after cleaning.

Methods

  • Leak detection equipment

    Checks for hidden leaks in the drain line after cleaning. A slow leak under a slab or in a wall shows up before it causes damage.

  • Camera-first inspection

    On any drain that has clogged more than once, we run the camera before we clean. Seeing the pipe tells us whether to snake, jet, or recommend repair. It also gives you a record of the line's condition.

  • Hydro jetting for grease and roots

    We jet lines with heavy grease or root intrusion because a snake only pokes a hole through the blockage. Jetting scours the pipe walls and restores full flow. We camera the line first to confirm the pipe can handle the pressure.

  • Root cutting with a rooter head

    Tree roots enter clay laterals at the joints. We run a rooter head through the line to cut them out, then jet the line to flush the debris. If the roots have cracked the pipe, we quote a spot repair.

  • Code-correct cleanout access

    We open the line at the cleanout whenever possible, not through a fixture. That protects your sink, tub, and toilet from damage and keeps the work compliant with Illinois plumbing code.

  • Multiple drains backing upMultiple drains backing up at onceWhen the kitchen, bathroom, and laundry all back up together, the main sewer line is blocked.
